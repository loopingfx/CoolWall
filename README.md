# CoolWall 🧊

> **The Ultimate Profiling & Coolness App**, inspired by the BBC television show *Top Gear*.

In *Top Gear*, Jeremy Clarkson and Richard Hammond debated cars on a giant magnetic studio wall, sorting them into:
- **Seriously Uncool**
- **Uncool**
- **Cool**
- **Sub-Zero**
- **The DB9 Fridge** (reserved for machines too breathtaking for standard wall physics)
- **The Ceiling / Out of Reach** (where Clarkson stuck cars so high Hammond couldn't reach without a stepladder)

**CoolWall** brings this concept to life as a modern, interactive web application for profiling and categorizing cars, billionaires, SpaceX rockets, Silicon Valley tech startups, cyberpunk icons, or anything else you want to judge!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Multi-Theme System**:
  - 🏎️ **Top Gear Classics**: The original studio board with supercars, hot hatches, and banter.
  - 💎 **Billionaires & Moguls**: Rank tech titans, oligarchs, superyachts, and $2,000 gold steaks from *Seriously Broke* to *Giga Sub-Zero*.
  - 🚀 **SpaceX & Aerospace**: Categorize rockets from *Rapid Unscheduled Disassembly (R.U.D.)* to *Mars Colony Sub-Zero*.
  - ⚡ **Silicon Valley & AI**: From *Deprecated Vaporware* to *Product-Market Fit* and *The AGI Singularity*.
  - 🦾 **Cyberpunk 2077**: From *Corpo Trash* to *Street Scum* and *Chrome Sub-Zero*.

- **Interactive Drag & Drop**:
  - Physically drag cards across the columns with magnetic snap and tilt physics.
  - Quick-shift buttons (← and →) for touchscreens and rapid sorting.
  - Sound effects synthesized in real time via the native Web Audio API (engine revs, rocket burns, cash register cha-ching, icy chimes, card slaps, and fail buzzers).

- **The DB9 Fridge & Cryo Vault**:
  - A dedicated refrigerated drawer operating at -273.15°C (Absolute Zero) for ultra-elite profiles.

- **Hammond's Stepladder Mode**:
  - Stick cards up high out of Hammond's reach, then pull out the stepladder with a click to retrieve them.

- **Top Gear "Cool Duel" Debate Game**:
  - Head-to-head face-off mode! Clarkson and Hammond put two profiles in the arena; you vote on which is cooler, shifting their standing with cheering sound effects and confetti!

- **Profile Maker & Editor**:
  - Upload your own photos, choose curated presets, or enter image URLs.
  - Coolness score slider with live status descriptions.
  - 🎲 **Witty Clarkson Quip Generator**: Generates authentic, hilarious Top Gear style verdicts.
  - Customizable performance telemetry, pros & cons, and tag badges.

- **Export & Backup**:
  - **Image Snapshot**: Render and download a high-res 1600x900 PNG wallpaper of your Cool Wall.
  - **JSON Backup & Import**: Save your profiles to JSON or share with friends.

- **React 19** + **TypeScript**

- **Lucide Icons**
- **Canvas-Confetti**
